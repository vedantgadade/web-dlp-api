VGSAVE FAQ

Clear answers about using VGSAVE, available qualities, MP3 conversion, troubleshooting, and responsible downloading.

Using VGSAVE

How do I download a file?

Paste a public media URL on the home page, choose an option shown after detection, then download the prepared file.

Which download options are available?

VGSAVE displays MP4 quality options based on the detected source and an MP3 option for compatible media. It does not promise a particular quality for every URL.

Is MP3 available?

Yes, MP3 is offered after compatible media is detected. The current target is 192 kbps, although the final audio quality is still limited by the source.

Video quality and files

Does VGSAVE always provide 1080p or 4K?

No. The source determines what can be offered. If a source does not provide usable 1080p or 2160p media, VGSAVE should not claim that quality is available.

Why can a higher quality take longer?

Higher-resolution media is often larger. Preparing and transferring a larger file can take longer and can use more storage and data.

Do I need an app?

No. VGSAVE is designed to work in a normal browser on supported phones and computers.

Can I download 1080p or 4K?

Only when the source provides a compatible format at that quality. VGSAVE does not manufacture missing source quality.

Where does the downloaded file go?

The file is handled by your browser using its normal download controls and storage location.

Does VGSAVE need my social-media password?

No. VGSAVE is intended for supported public links and does not ask you to bypass private access controls.

When a link does not work

Why did detection or download fail?

The link may be private, unavailable, unsupported, restricted by the source, or temporarily inaccessible. Confirm that it is a public URL and try again later.

Can I use any link?

No. Use only public links to material you own or are authorized to download. See the Terms of Use and responsible downloading guide.

Can VGSAVE bypass a private video?

No. VGSAVE is intended for public media and does not provide a way to bypass private access controls.

Privacy and responsible use

Should I download copyrighted material?

Only when you have the rights or permission required for the intended use. Public visibility does not automatically remove copyright or licensing restrictions.

Where can I read the site's policies?

See the Privacy Policy, Terms, Copyright / DMCA, and Disclaimer.
