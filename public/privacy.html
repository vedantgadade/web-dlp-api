Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited information VGSAVE handles while providing its download service.

Submitted links

VGSAVE processes the public URL you submit to detect and prepare media. Do not submit private or sensitive URLs.

Temporary files

Prepared files and related job records are temporary and are automatically removed after a limited retention period.

Analytics

VGSAVE uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as which pages are visited and basic browser or device information. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. This information is provided through Google Analytics and is used to understand site usage rather than to ask for passwords or private account credentials.

Advertising and cookies

VGSAVE's advertising setup is subject to the site's advertising review and configuration. While review is in progress, advertising may not be served. If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to help serve, measure, and personalize ads where permitted. Visitors can manage available advertising and cookie choices through their browser or Google settings, where available.

VGSAVE does not ask for social-media passwords and is not intended to collect private account credentials through the downloader.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, use the Contact page. The project will publish its dedicated support contact method there before accepting formal privacy requests.
