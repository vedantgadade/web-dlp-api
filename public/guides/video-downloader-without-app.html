VGSAVE guide

Video downloader without app guide

VGSAVE runs in a normal browser. Paste a supported public URL, detect formats, choose an available option, and download the prepared file.

How to use VGSAVE

  1. Copy the URL of a public video you own or are authorized to download.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste the URL.
  3. Select Get download options.
  4. Choose an available option returned for the source.
  5. Wait for the file to be prepared, then download it.

What affects the result?

VGSAVE works from the media formats made available by the source. It cannot invent a missing resolution, restore unavailable source detail, or bypass private access controls.

Common limitations

  • The URL may be private, removed, restricted, or temporarily unavailable.
  • A requested quality may not exist for the source.
  • Higher-quality files can be larger and take longer to prepare.
  • Source platforms can change how public media is delivered.

If something fails, try the download troubleshooting guide.

Use a browser

VGSAVE is designed to work from a normal browser. You do not need to install a separate downloader app to use the website.
