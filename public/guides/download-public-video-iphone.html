iPhone & iPad guide

How to download a supported public video on iPhone.

Use VGSAVE in a mobile browser to check a supported public URL and save an available file to your device.

Steps on iPhone or iPad

  1. Copy the URL of a public video you own or are authorized to download.
  2. Open Safari or another browser and visit VGSAVE.
  3. Paste the URL and tap Get download options.
  4. Choose an available MP4 quality or MP3 option.
  5. Wait for processing and tap the download button when the file is ready.
  6. Use the browser's download controls or the Files app to locate the saved file.

Choose a sensible quality

Higher resolution usually means a larger file. Choose a quality that matches your screen, storage, and data connection instead of automatically choosing the largest option.

If the download fails

Confirm that the source is public and still available. Private media, removed content, unsupported URLs, and temporary source or network problems can prevent processing.

Use public media responsibly

Only download material you own or have permission to download. Respect copyright, privacy, licensing, and source-platform rules.

For more information, read the video download guide and responsible downloading guide.
