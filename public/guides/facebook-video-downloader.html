Facebook Video Downloader Guide

Learn how to use VGSAVE with supported public Facebook video links, choose available quality, and troubleshoot common download problems. The workflow is intended for public content you own or are authorized to download.

How to use VGSAVE

  1. Copy a public video URL you are authorized to download.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste it.
  3. Select Get download options.
  4. Review the qualities returned for the source.
  5. Choose an available option and download the prepared file.

Quality depends on the source

VGSAVE can only offer formats made available by the source. It does not invent missing 1080p or 4K quality or bypass private access controls.

See the video quality guide and 4K guide.

Common limitations

  • The video may be private, removed, restricted, or temporarily unavailable.
  • The source may not currently provide the requested format.
  • Higher-quality files can be larger and take longer to prepare.
  • Source platforms can change how public media is delivered.

See download troubleshooting if detection or preparation fails.

Mobile and desktop

VGSAVE works in a browser on supported phones, tablets, and computers. Large files may require additional storage and data.

Responsible use

Only download content you own or have permission to download. Respect copyright, privacy, and platform rules.
