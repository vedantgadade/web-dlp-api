Instagram video downloader guide

Learn how to check supported public Instagram video links, see available quality, and save a video you own or are authorized to download.

How to download a supported public Instagram video

  1. Open the public Instagram video you are authorized to save and copy its link.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste the URL.
  3. Select Get download options.
  4. Review the qualities returned for that source.
  5. Select an available option and download the prepared file.

Instagram Reels and regular videos

VGSAVE has a dedicated Instagram Reels downloader guide. The exact options shown depend on the media delivered by the source URL.

Why is a quality missing?

VGSAVE shows formats that can be obtained from the supported source. If the original media does not provide a particular resolution, that resolution cannot be created from the source. A source can also change how its public media is delivered.

For resolution and file-size information, see the video quality guide and 4K video downloader guide.

Can private Instagram videos be downloaded?

No. This guide is for supported public media that you own or are authorized to download. VGSAVE is not intended to bypass private accounts, login requirements, or access controls.

Using VGSAVE on a phone

VGSAVE works through a normal browser. After preparing a file, use your browser's normal download and file-management controls. Large video files can require significant storage and mobile data.

Common problems

  • Link is not detected: confirm that the URL is complete and publicly accessible.
  • Video was removed: the source must still be available.
  • Only lower quality appears: the source may not provide a higher-quality format.
  • Download fails: try detection again or another available quality and check the troubleshooting guide.
