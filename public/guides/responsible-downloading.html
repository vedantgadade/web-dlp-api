Responsible use

Download public media responsibly.

Publicly accessible does not automatically mean free of copyright, licensing, privacy, or other restrictions.

Public does not always mean reusable

A video can be visible without a login and still belong to its creator or another rights holder. Before saving or reusing a file, consider whether you own it, have permission, or have a license that allows the intended use.

Before downloading

  • Make sure you own the content or have permission to download it.
  • Check the copyright and licensing terms that apply to the media.
  • Respect privacy and do not use downloaded material to harass, impersonate, or expose another person.
  • Follow applicable laws and the source platform's rules.
  • If you plan to publish or commercially reuse the file, verify that the permission or license covers that use.

What VGSAVE does not do

VGSAVE is not intended to bypass private access controls, account restrictions, paywalls, or other protections that limit access to content. A failed request should not be treated as an invitation to circumvent the source's restrictions.

If you own copyrighted material

If you believe content is being used improperly, review the Copyright / DMCA page for the available contact process. Providing clear information helps a rights holder or site operator review a complaint.

Safer ways to share downloaded files

When you have permission to share a file, keep the original creator or license information where appropriate. Do not remove attribution or copyright notices when a license requires them.

Use the downloader

When you have the necessary rights or permission, return to the VGSAVE downloader. If you are unsure about a particular use, check the applicable license or obtain permission before downloading or publishing the material.
