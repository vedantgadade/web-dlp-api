Responsible use

Download public media responsibly.

Publicly accessible does not automatically mean free of copyright or other restrictions.

Before downloading

  • Make sure you own the content or have permission to download it.
  • Check the copyright and licensing terms that apply to the media.
  • Respect privacy and do not use downloaded material to harass, impersonate, or expose another person.
  • Follow applicable laws and the source platform's rules.

What VGSAVE does not do

VGSAVE is not intended to bypass private access controls, account restrictions, or other protections that limit access to content.

If you own copyrighted material

If you believe content is being used improperly, review the Copyright / DMCA page for the available contact process.

Use the downloader

When you have the necessary rights or permission, return to the VGSAVE downloader.
