Instagram Story downloader guide

Learn what to expect when checking a supported public Instagram Story link with VGSAVE.

How to check a public Story

  1. Copy the link to a public Instagram Story you own or are authorized to save.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste the URL.
  3. Select Get download options.
  4. Choose one of the qualities returned for the source.
  5. Download the prepared file using your browser.

Why a Story may not be available

Stories are temporary media and may disappear or become unavailable before a link is processed. A link can also require access that VGSAVE does not provide. VGSAVE does not bypass private accounts, login requirements, or access controls.

What quality will I get?

The available quality depends on the source media. VGSAVE cannot create a higher-quality original when the source does not provide it. See the video quality guide for an explanation of resolution and file size.

Phone and desktop use

The service runs in a browser, so you can use it from a phone, tablet, or computer. Large videos can use significant storage and mobile data.

Story vs Reels and videos

For Instagram Reels, use the Instagram Reels downloader guide. For other public Instagram videos, see the Instagram video downloader guide.

Common problems

  • Detection fails: verify that the URL is complete and publicly accessible.
  • Story expired: the source may no longer expose the media.
  • Quality is lower than expected: the source may not provide a higher-quality format.
  • Download fails: try detection again or another available quality, then check troubleshooting.

Use responsibly

Only save content you own or have permission to download. Respect privacy, copyright, and Instagram's rules.
