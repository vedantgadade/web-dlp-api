Instagram public video guide

How to download a public Instagram video with VGSAVE.

For supported public Instagram media, VGSAVE checks the source and shows the options it can actually process.

Steps

  1. Open the public Instagram video or reel you are authorized to download and copy its URL.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste the link.
  3. Select Get download options to check the source.
  4. Choose an available MP4 quality or MP3 when it is offered.
  5. Wait for the file to finish preparing, then download it.

Why can an Instagram link fail?

The media may be private, deleted, region-limited, temporarily inaccessible, or changed by the source platform. A URL that looks public can also require access that VGSAVE cannot use. VGSAVE does not bypass private accounts or access controls.

Why can the available quality vary?

The source determines which media streams can be processed. VGSAVE shows available choices rather than promising a fixed resolution for every reel or video.

Respect creators and privacy

Only download media you own or are authorized to download. Do not use saved videos to impersonate, harass, expose private information, or violate a creator's rights. Check the source's rules and any applicable license before reusing content.

See the video download guide, quality guide, and responsible downloading guide for more information.
