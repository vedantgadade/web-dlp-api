MP4 video downloader guide

Understand MP4 downloads, choose a suitable quality, and avoid common problems when working with supported public video links.

MP4 is a common video file format that works with many phones, computers, browsers, and media players. An online MP4 downloader can prepare a video file from a supported public link so you can save it locally for an authorized use.

How to download a video as MP4

  1. Open the VGSAVE downloader.
  2. Paste a supported public video URL.
  3. Select Get download options.
  4. Review the MP4 qualities returned for that source.
  5. Choose the quality that fits your needs and available storage.
  6. Wait for preparation to finish and download the file.

Which MP4 quality should you choose?

The best choice depends on the source, screen size, storage, and internet connection. Lower resolutions usually create smaller files and can be convenient on limited storage or mobile data. Higher resolutions can look better on large displays but generally require more storage and data.

QualityTypical use
144p–360pSmall files and low-bandwidth viewing
480pBasic viewing on smaller screens
720pGeneral HD viewing
1080pFull HD viewing when the source provides it
1440p–2160pHigher-resolution viewing when the source provides it

These labels describe resolution, not a promise about visual quality. The original source, compression, frame rate, and encoding also affect the result. See the video quality guide.

Why a 4K MP4 option may not appear

VGSAVE cannot add detail that is missing from the source. If a public video is available only at lower resolutions, higher options should not be expected. Source platforms can also change the formats they make available.

Why MP4 downloads can take time

A larger video file can require more processing and transfer time. A higher-resolution file can also use substantially more storage. If preparation stays stuck or fails, check the troubleshooting guide and try the public URL again later.

Phone or computer?

VGSAVE works through a normal browser for supported public links. For device-specific steps, see the Android guide, iPhone and iPad guide, or PC guide.

MP4 download and copyright

Use an MP4 downloader only for content you own or are authorized to download. Public visibility does not automatically give permission to copy, redistribute, or reuse copyrighted material. Do not use VGSAVE to bypass private access controls.
