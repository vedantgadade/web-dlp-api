Video download guide

How to download a supported public video.

VGSAVE checks a public URL first and shows the options it can actually offer. This guide explains the process and the most common reasons a request may fail.

Before you start

Use a normal public URL for media you own or are authorized to download. A link being visible on the internet does not automatically give everyone permission to copy or reuse the media. If the source requires a login or other private access, VGSAVE is not intended to bypass it.

Steps

  1. Copy the URL of a public video you are allowed to download.
  2. Open the VGSAVE downloader and paste the URL.
  3. Select Get download options. VGSAVE checks the source before showing choices.
  4. Choose one of the video qualities returned by the source check, or choose MP3 when it is offered.
  5. Wait while the file is prepared. When processing finishes, use the download button shown by VGSAVE.

Why can a public link fail?

A public URL can stop working for several reasons. The original media may have been deleted, the source may have changed its delivery system, the media may be unavailable in your region, or the URL may actually require an account. Temporary network or service errors can also occur.

If the same link fails repeatedly, check that it still opens normally in your browser. If it does not open publicly there, VGSAVE cannot make it public.

Why are some qualities missing?

Quality choices are based on the source media. A 1080p or 4K button should not be assumed to exist when the source does not provide usable media at that level. VGSAVE avoids pretending that a missing source quality exists.

What if the download is slower than expected?

Preparation time depends on the source, selected quality, file size, and current server workload. Higher-resolution files are generally larger and can take longer to prepare. Keep the browser tab open until the result is ready.
