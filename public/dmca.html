Copyright and DMCA

VGSAVE respects intellectual-property rights and provides a process for submitting complete copyright notices.

Submitting a notice

Include identification of the copyrighted work, the relevant public URL, your contact details, a good-faith statement, an accuracy statement under penalty of perjury, and your physical or electronic signature.

How to start a copyright request

Review the required notice information above, then use the Contact page to request the current private submission channel. Do not publish confidential legal or personal information in a public issue or other public forum.
