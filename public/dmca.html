Copyright and DMCA

VGSAVE respects intellectual-property rights and will review complete copyright notices when a contact method is configured.

Submitting a notice

Include identification of the copyrighted work, the relevant public URL, your contact details, a good-faith statement, an accuracy statement under penalty of perjury, and your physical or electronic signature.

Contact required before launch

A designated copyright contact email and process must be added before launch. Until then, do not represent that requests can be received or acted upon through this site.
